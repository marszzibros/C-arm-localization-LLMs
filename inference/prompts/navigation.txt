GEOMETRIC NAVIGATION CLASSIFIER FOR SIMULATED C-ARM X-RAY IMAGES

You MUST follow all instructions below and output VALID XML ONLY.  
Do NOT output any text outside the XML structure.

============================================================
TASK OVERVIEW
============================================================
You will perform TWO STEPS:

STEP 1:
Identify WHICH SINGLE LANDMARK is closest to the image center.

STEP 2:
Given the current closest landmark (context) and a USER TARGET landmark,
decide how to MOVE THE VIEW so the TARGET landmark moves toward the image center.

All directions are in the PATIENT’S ANATOMICAL FRAME:
- LEFT / RIGHT = anatomical left / right
- UP = cranial / superior
- DOWN = caudal / inferior

You are describing movement of the VIEW, not movement of the anatomy.

============================================================
LANDMARK LIST (exact names)
============================================================
You MUST use these exact names:

1. Skull
2. Right humeral head
3. Left humeral head
4. Right scapular inferior angle
5. Left scapular inferior angle
6. Right elbow
7. Left elbow
8. Right wrist
9. Left wrist
10. T1
11. Carina
12. Right hemidiaphragm
13. Left hemidiaphragm
14. T12

The USER TARGET ID will always be one of 1–14 from this list.

============================================================
STEP 1 — VIEW CONTEXT (SINGLE CLOSEST LANDMARK)
============================================================
Choose the ONE landmark that is closest to the image center.

Output:

<step1>
  <context>LANDMARK_NAME</context>
</step1>

Rules:
- <context> MUST be exactly one of the 14 names above
- Output exactly ONE name
- Do not output IDs or multiple landmarks

============================================================
STEP 2 — MOVEMENT DECISION (ANATOMICAL FRAME)
============================================================
Inputs:
- context landmark from Step 1
- target landmark from the 1–14 list

Goal:
- determine the anatomical direction of the TARGET relative to the current view center
- decide how to MOVE THE VIEW so the TARGET moves toward the image center

Allowed tokens:

<x> must be exactly one of:
- LEFT
- RIGHT
- CENTER

<y> must be exactly one of:
- UP
- DOWN
- CENTER

Extent tokens (both axes):
- NONE
- SMALL
- MODERATE
- LARGE

Extent rules:
- If direction = CENTER → extent MUST be NONE
- If direction ≠ CENTER → extent MUST NOT be NONE

Direction rules:
- target toward anatomical right  → x = RIGHT
- target toward anatomical left   → x = LEFT
- target is superior              → y = UP
- target is inferior              → y = DOWN
- if already approximately aligned → CENTER

Output format:

<step2>
  <x>LEFT|RIGHT|CENTER</x>
  <extent_x>NONE|SMALL|MODERATE|LARGE</extent_x>
  <reason_x>...</reason_x>
  <y>UP|DOWN|CENTER</y>
  <extent_y>NONE|SMALL|MODERATE|LARGE</extent_y>
  <reason_y>...</reason_y>
</step2>

Any other value makes the output INVALID.

============================================================
CORE ANATOMICAL ORDER (SUPERIOR → INFERIOR)
============================================================
You MUST respect the following vertical anatomical order:

Skull  
T1  
Carina  
Right scapular inferior angle / Left scapular inferior angle  
Hemidiaphragms  
T12  

Therefore:

- Skull is ALWAYS superior to T1
- T1 is ALWAYS inferior to Skull
- Carina is inferior to T1
- Both the Right scapular inferior angle and the Left scapular inferior angle are inferior to T1
- Hemidiaphragms are inferior to Carina
- T12 is inferior to the hemidiaphragms

============================================================
ANATOMICAL KNOWLEDGE TO USE (LATERAL & DISTAL)
============================================================
You may also assume:

- Right-sided landmarks are on anatomical right
- Left-sided landmarks are on anatomical left
- Humeral heads are lateral to the thoracic spine
- Elbows are distal/inferior to humeral heads
- Wrists are distal/inferior to elbows
- Scapular inferior angles are inferior to humeral heads
- Skull is located in center

ADDITIONAL RELATIONS FOR SCAPULAR INFERIOR ANGLES AND Skull:

- Both scapular inferior angles (Right and Left) are located clearly inferior (caudal) to Skull.
- Skull is always superior to both scapular inferior angles.
- Therefore, relative to Skull:
  - The Right scapular inferior angle is inferior AND to the anatomical right.
  - The Left scapular inferior angle is inferior AND to the anatomical left.

============================================================
REASONING REQUIREMENTS
============================================================
For BOTH reason_x and reason_y:

You MUST:
- explicitly name BOTH the context landmark and the target landmark
- clearly describe their relationship on that axis
  - left/right for x
  - superior/inferior (above/below) for y
  - or aligned/centered if CENTER is chosen

You MUST ensure:
- the textual reason matches the chosen direction token
- the reason does NOT contradict the direction
- Skull vs T1 relation is NOT described as “same level” or “aligned”

You may vary wording freely:
- short or long sentences
- “above/below” or “superior/inferior”
- “shift/move/adjust” etc.

Examples of acceptable (not mandatory):

x-axis:
- “Relative to the context T1, the target Left humeral head is farther to the anatomical left, so the view should move left.”
- “The target Right elbow lies on the anatomical right compared with the context Carina, so a rightward shift is appropriate.”
- “The target T1 and the context Skull are essentially aligned left–right, so no horizontal movement is needed.”

y-axis:
- “The target T1 is inferior to the Skull, so the view should move down.”
- “Compared to the context Right humeral head, the target Right wrist is positioned inferiorly, so a downward adjustment is needed.”
- “The target Left humeral head is at a similar vertical level to the context Right humeral head, so no vertical change is required.”
